What are dental implants?

A permanent tooth replacement that looks, feels & works like your own

A dental implant is a small, biocompatible titanium post placed in the jawbone. It fuses with the bone and supports a custom crown, bridge or denture, restoring your smile without touching neighbouring teeth.

  • Lifelong solution with proper care and regular check-ups
  • Preserves jawbone and keeps your face shape youthful
  • Eat & speak confidently with no slipping like removable dentures
  • Natural aesthetics with crowns shade-matched to your teeth

Implant Treatments

Implant solutions for every situation

From one missing tooth to a full mouth, each treatment is planned around your bone, bite and smile goals.

Single Tooth Implant

Replace one missing tooth with an implant and crown, no grinding of adjacent healthy teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridge

Two or more implants support a fixed bridge to replace several adjacent missing teeth.

All-on-4 / Full Arch

A complete fixed set of teeth on just four to six implants, often in fewer visits.

Implant Overdentures

Snap-on dentures secured by implants for a stable, comfortable fit without adhesives.

Immediate Implants

Where suitable, the implant is placed on the same day the tooth is removed, saving time.

Bone Grafting & Sinus Lift

Rebuilds lost bone so implants can be placed safely even after long-term tooth loss.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of a dental implant in Karachi?

The cost depends on the implant system, the type of crown and whether bone grafting is needed. After your consultation and X-ray you'll receive a clear, itemised estimate. Contact us to book an assessment.

Is the implant procedure painful?

Implant placement is done under local anaesthesia, so you shouldn't feel pain during the procedure. Most patients report only mild discomfort afterwards, which is easily managed with routine pain relief.

How long does the whole treatment take?

Typically 2–4 months from placement to final crown, allowing the implant to fuse with the bone. In suitable cases, immediate implants and temporary teeth can shorten this time.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, implants can last for decades and often a lifetime. The crown may need replacement after many years of normal wear.

Am I a suitable candidate for implants?

Most healthy adults with missing teeth are candidates. Conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking need extra care. Bone loss can often be corrected with grafting. A consultation will confirm what's right for you.

What are "American" implants?

This refers to premium, FDA-approved implant systems manufactured in the USA, known for strong research backing, reliable success rates and long-term availability of parts.
